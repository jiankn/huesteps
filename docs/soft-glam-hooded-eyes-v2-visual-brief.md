# Soft Glam for Hooded Eyes — progressive visual brief v2

## Shared art direction

- Asset: eight separate instructional beauty photographs for the web tutorial, not a contact sheet.
- Subject: hooded lids, brown eyes, dark natural brows, light-medium neutral-warm beige skin.
- Identity: the same person is preferred but not required. Skin depth and undertone are mandatory invariants across all eight images.
- Comparison view: straight-on relaxed open eyes, compact crop from the lower forehead to just above the nose tip, both eyes visible, 4:3 landscape.
- Light and color: soft even daylight, neutral background, accurate white balance, no warm/cool color drift.
- Detail: high depth of field; natural pores, eyelid lines, fine facial hair, slight under-eye texture, real powder and satin pigment visible.
- Exclude: beauty smoothing, plastic skin, blur, excessive sharpening, text, watermark, hands/tools, false lashes, anatomical artifacts, mismatched eye direction, or a different skin undertone.
- Delivery: one high-resolution source per step; reviewed 1280 × 960 WebP at quality 92.

| 8 | Clean, symmetrical final open-eye balance; no added product weight | Steps 1–7 | New color, thicker liner, heavier mascara, retouched skin |

## Review checklist

Reject and regenerate if any answer is no:

1. Can a viewer identify the new change without reading the caption?
2. Is every later-step effect absent from the earlier image?
3. Does the skin depth and undertone match the rest of the set?
4. Are pores, lid folds, fine texture, and pigment visible at original size?
5. Are both eyes anatomically plausible, aligned, and in focus?
6. Is the taupe/rose/cobalt color placement consistent with the tutorial text?
7. Does the 4:3 crop remain useful at mobile and desktop widths?
8. Is the image free of baked-in text, watermarks, tools, and hands?
